Section 02

Deep-Dive Core Concepts & Teacher Explanations

1. Similarity in Change & Aspect Ratios

When you enlarge a digital photo measuring 6 cm × 4 cm to a width of 18 cm, what should the new height be?
To prevent the photo from being squished, the ratio of length to width must remain constant:

Original Ratio = 6 / 4 = 3 / 2
New Ratio = 18 / H = 3 / 2 ⇒ 3H = 36 ⇒ H = 12 cm.

Here, the scaling factor is 18 ÷ 6 = 3. Both dimensions multiplied by 3!

2. Direct Variation (Proportionality)

Definition of Direct Proportion: Two quantities x and y are said to be in direct proportion if they increase (or decrease) together such that the ratio of their corresponding values remains constant:
x / y = k    (or x₁ / y₁ = x₂ / y₂) where k is a positive constant called the constant of proportionality.

3. Dividing a Quantity in a Given Ratio

Suppose an amount T is to be shared between two persons A and B in the ratio m : n:

Total parts = m + n
A's share = (m / (m + n)) × T
B's share = (n / (m + n)) × T
Example (Paint Blending): To create green paint, blue and yellow paints are mixed in the ratio 3 : 5. If 40 litres of green paint are needed:
Total parts = 3 + 5 = 8 parts.
Blue paint = (3 / 8) × 40 = 15 litres.
Yellow paint = (5 / 8) × 40 = 25 litres.
Section 03

In-Text Activities & Classroom Explorations (Fully Solved)

Activity 1 (Page 6): Neelima's mother's age is 3 times her age. What is the ratio of Neelima's age to her mother's age? What will the ratio be after 10 years?

Present: Let Neelima's age be x. Her mother's age = 3x.

Ratio of Neelima's age to mother's age = x : 3x = 1 : 3.

After 10 years: Neelima's age = x + 10; Mother's age = 3x + 10.

New ratio = (x + 10) / (3x + 10). Notice that this ratio is NOT 1 : 3! For example, if Neelima is 10, mother is 30 (1:3). After 10 years, ages are 20 and 40 (ratio becomes 1:2!).

Lesson: Adding a constant number does NOT preserve proportionality!

Activity 2 (Page 12): 200 g of tea costs ₹200, while 500 g costs ₹450. Are weight and price directly proportional?

Check the Ratios:

  • For 200 g: Price per gram = 200 / 200 = ₹1 per gram.
  • For 500 g: Price per gram = 450 / 500 = ₹0.90 per gram.

Conclusion: Since the ratio of price to weight is not constant (200/200 ≠ 450/500), they are NOT strictly directly proportional (a bulk discount was applied!).

Section 04

Complete End-of-Chapter 'Figure it Out' Exercises (Solved Step-by-Step)

Exercise 1: If 15 metres of uniform cloth costs ₹450, what will be the cost of 28 metres of the same cloth?
Identify: Length of cloth and cost are in direct proportion (more cloth = more cost).
Proportion: x₁ / y₁ = x₂ / y₂ ⇒ 15 / 450 = 28 / y₂.
Cross-multiply: 15 × y₂ = 450 × 28.
Solve: y₂ = (450 × 28) / 15 = 30 × 28 = 840.
Result: The cost of 28 metres of cloth is ₹840.
Exercise 2: A map is drawn with a scale of 1 cm = 25 km. Two cities are 3.6 cm apart on the map. What is the actual distance between them?
Scale Factor: 1 cm on map corresponds to 25 km in real life.
Given Map Distance: 3.6 cm.
Real Distance: 3.6 × 25 km.
Calculation: 3.6 × 25 = 90 km.
Result: The actual distance is 90 km.
Exercise 3: Divide ₹1,200 between Rohit and Sumit in the ratio 5 : 3.
Total Ratio: 5 + 3 = 8 parts.
Value of 1 part: 1200 ÷ 8 = ₹150.
Rohit's share (5 parts): 5 × 150 = ₹750.
Sumit's share (3 parts): 3 × 150 = ₹450.
Result: Rohit gets ₹750 and Sumit gets ₹450.
Section 05

Interactive Practice Quiz Engine

Score: 0 / 5 Answer the questions below to test your mastery!

1. In direct proportion, if x increases by a factor of 4, what happens to y?

Teacher Explanation
Correct Answer: (a).
In direct proportion, the ratio y/x is constant. If x is multiplied by 4, y must also be multiplied by 4.

2. An aspect ratio of 16 : 9 means that for every 16 units of width, there are:

Teacher Explanation
Correct Answer: (b) 9 units of height.
Standard widescreen TV and phone displays follow a 16:9 width-to-height ratio.

3. If 8 identical notebooks cost ₹120, how much will 20 notebooks cost?

Teacher Explanation
Correct Answer: (c) ₹300.
1 notebook costs 120 ÷ 8 = ₹15. So 20 notebooks cost 20 × 15 = ₹300.

4. Two quantities x and y vary directly. When x = 12, y = 36. What is y when x = 7?

Teacher Explanation
Correct Answer: (a) 21.
Constant k = y/x = 36/12 = 3. So when x = 7, y = 3 × 7 = 21.

5. A 5 m tall pole casts a shadow of 3 m. At the same time, what height of building casts a 15 m shadow?

Teacher Explanation
Correct Answer: (d) 25 m.
Height and shadow length vary directly: 5 / 3 = H / 15 ⇒ H = (5 × 15) / 3 = 25 m.
Section 06

Quick Revision Notes & Common Pitfalls

Pitfall: Assuming all increasing relationships are proportional!
A child gets taller every year, but height is NOT directly proportional to age (a 20-year-old is not 10 times taller than a 2-year-old!).
Direct proportion requires the RATIO y/x to remain strictly constant.
