Section 02

Deep-Dive Core Concepts & Teacher Explanations

1. What is Inverse Proportionality?

Definition: Two quantities x and y are in inverse proportion if an increase in x causes a proportional decrease in y such that their product remains constant:
x × y = k      (or x₁y₁ = x₂y₂) where k is a non-zero constant.

2. Direct vs Inverse Proportion: The Showdown

Feature Direct Proportion Inverse Proportion
Behaviour Both increase together (or decrease together) One increases while the other decreases
Constant Entity Ratio is constant: x / y = k Product is constant: x × y = k
Formula x₁ / y₁ = x₂ / y₂ x₁ × y₁ = x₂ × y₂
Classic Example Distance covered and petrol consumed Speed of vehicle and travel time for fixed trip

3. Everyday Mechanical Examples of Inverse Proportion

A. Gear Wheels in Bicycles & Clocks:
When two meshed gears turn:
(Number of Teeth on Gear A) × (Revolutions of A) = (Number of Teeth on Gear B) × (Revolutions of B)
A smaller gear with half as many teeth must spin twice as fast!
B. Water Pumps & Tank Fill Times:
More pipes running = faster filling time!
(Number of Pumps) × (Hours to Fill) = Constant Capacity
Section 03

In-Text Activities & Classroom Explorations (Fully Solved)

Activity 1 (Page 10): Going by bicycle is 3 times faster than walking (15 km/h vs 5 km/h). If walking takes 60 minutes, how long will cycling take?

Concept: Speed and Time are in inverse proportion for a fixed distance.

Speed₁ × Time₁ = Speed₂ × Time₂

5 km/h × 60 min = 15 km/h × Time₂

Time₂ = (5 × 60) ÷ 15 = 300 ÷ 15 = 20 minutes.

Notice: Speed multiplied by 3, so time divided by 3 (60 ÷ 3 = 20 min).

Activity 2 (Page 12): 2 pumps can fill a water tank in 18 hours. How much time will it take if 6 identical pumps are used?

Step 1: Identify: More pumps = less time (inverse proportion).

Step 2: Formula: Pumps₁ × Time₁ = Pumps₂ × Time₂.

Step 3: 2 × 18 = 6 × Time₂ ⇒ 36 = 6 × Time₂.

Step 4: Time₂ = 36 ÷ 6 = 6 hours.

Result: 6 pumps will fill the tank in just 6 hours.

Section 04

Complete End-of-Chapter 'Figure it Out' Exercises (Solved Step-by-Step)

Exercise 1: If 15 workers can build a wall in 48 hours, how many workers will be required to do the same work in 30 hours?
Identify: Workers (W) and Time (T) are in inverse proportion: W₁ × T₁ = W₂ × T₂.
Given: W₁ = 15, T₁ = 48, T₂ = 30, W₂ = ?
Equation: 15 × 48 = W₂ × 30.
Calculate: W₂ = (15 × 48) / 30 = 720 / 30 = 24.
Result: 24 workers are required to finish the work in 30 hours.
Exercise 2: A car takes 2 hours to reach a destination by travelling at the speed of 60 km/h. How long will it take when the car travels at the speed of 80 km/h?
Distance: Speed₁ × Time₁ = 60 × 2 = 120 km.
New Time: Time₂ = Distance / Speed₂ = 120 / 80 = 1.5 hours.
Result: It will take 1.5 hours (1 hour and 30 minutes).
Exercise 3: A gear with 24 teeth meshes with a smaller gear having 8 teeth. When the large gear completes 10 revolutions, how many revolutions does the smaller gear complete?
Gear Law: Teeth₁ × Rev₁ = Teeth₂ × Rev₂.
Substitute: 24 × 10 = 8 × Rev₂.
Solve: Rev₂ = 240 / 8 = 30 revolutions.
Result: The smaller gear completes 30 revolutions.

Interactive Practice Quiz Engine

Score: 0 / 5 Answer the questions below to test your mastery!

1. If two quantities x and y are in inverse proportion, which of the following is CONSTANT?

Teacher Explanation
Correct Answer: (b) x × y.
In inverse variation, the product of the two variables remains constant.

2. If 10 men can reap a field in 6 days, how many days will 15 men take?

Teacher Explanation
Correct Answer: (a) 4 days.
Total man-days = 10 × 6 = 60. For 15 men: 60 ÷ 15 = 4 days.

3. Which of the following is an example of inverse proportion?

Teacher Explanation
Correct Answer: (c).
As speed increases, time taken decreases proportionally. The other options are direct proportions.

4. A hostel has food provision for 50 students for 30 days. If 25 more students join, how long will the food last?

Teacher Explanation
Correct Answer: (b) 20 days.
Total student-days = 50 × 30 = 1500. Total students = 50 + 25 = 75. Days = 1500 ÷ 75 = 20 days.

5. If x and y vary inversely and x = 4 when y = 9, what is y when x = 6?

Teacher Explanation
Correct Answer: (c) 6.
x × y = 4 × 9 = 36. When x = 6: y = 36 ÷ 6 = 6.
Section 06

Quick Revision Notes & Common Pitfalls

Pitfall: Using cross-multiplication on an inverse proportion!
For inverse proportion, do NOT set x₁/y₁ = x₂/y₂!
Always set the PRODUCTS EQUAL: x₁ × y₁ = x₂ × y₂.
Proportion Type Constant Equation How to Solve Unknown
Directx / y = kx₂ = (x₁ × y₂) / y₁
Inversex × y = kx₂ = (x₁ × y₁) / y₂
