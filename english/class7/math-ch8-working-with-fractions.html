8.2 Reciprocals and Division

The reciprocal (or multiplicative inverse) of a non-zero fraction a/b is b/a.
When you multiply a fraction by its reciprocal, the product is always 1:

(a/b) × (b/a) = 1   |   Reciprocal of 4/7 is 7/4   |   Reciprocal of 5 is 1/5.
The Golden Rule of Fraction Division: "Keep, Change, Flip!"
• Keep the first fraction unchanged.
• Change the division sign (÷) to multiplication (×).
• Flip the second fraction to its reciprocal!
(a/b) ÷ (c/d) = (a/b) × (d/c).

Activity 1: Sridharacharya's 9th-Century Pāṭīgaṇita Problem (Page 25)

Historic Problem: In Pāṭīgaṇita (c. 9th century CE), Sridharacharya poses: "Friend, after thinking, what sum will be obtained by adding together: 1 ÷ ⅙, 1 ÷ &frac{1}{10}, 1 ÷ &frac{1}{13}, 1 ÷ &frac19;, and 1 ÷ ½?"

Concept: 1 ÷ (1/n) = 1 × (n/1) = n!
Evaluating each term:
• 1 ÷ ⅙ = 6
• 1 ÷ &frac{1}{10} = 10
• 1 ÷ &frac{1}{13} = 13
• 1 ÷ &frac19; = 9
• 1 ÷ ½ = 2
Adding together: 6 + 10 + 13 + 9 + 2 = 40!
✓ Answer: The friend should say 40!

Activity 2: Cross-Cancellation Multiplication (Page 11)

Evaluate: (14/15) × (25/42)

Step 1: Notice common factors between numerators and denominators:
• 14 and 42 share factor 14 ⇒ 14 ÷ 14 = 1, and 42 ÷ 14 = 3.
• 25 and 15 share factor 5 ⇒ 25 ÷ 5 = 5, and 15 ÷ 5 = 3.
Step 2: Multiply the reduced numbers:
(1 × 5) / (3 × 3) = 5/9.
✓ Final Answer: 5/9.

Q1. Maria's Lace Cutting Problem (Page 24)

Problem: Maria bought 8 m of lace to decorate dresses. Each dress requires ⅖ m of lace. How many dresses can she decorate?

Total Lace: 8 meters
Lace per Dress: ⅖ meter
Number of Dresses: 8 ÷ (⅖) = 8 × (5/2) = (8 × 5) ÷ 2 = 40 ÷ 2 = 20.
✓ Answer: Maria can decorate 20 dresses.
Q2. Flour for Rotis (Page 25)

Problem: If ¼ kg of flour is used to make 12 rotis, how much flour is used to make 6 rotis?

Observation: 6 rotis is exactly half of 12 rotis (6 ÷ 12 = ½).
Calculation: ½ of ¼ kg = ½ × ¼ = ⅛ kg (or 125 grams).
✓ Answer: ⅛ kg (125 g) of flour is used to make 6 rotis.
Q3. Mariam's Cake Sharing (Page 25, 38)

Problem: Mariam and her 3 cousins finished 3/4 of a cake equally. What fraction of the whole cake did each person eat?

Total People: Mariam + 3 cousins = 4 people.
Portion Eaten: 3/4 of the cake divided equally among 4 people.
Calculation: (3/4) ÷ 4 = (3/4) × (1/4) = 3/16.
✓ Answer: Each person ate 3/16 of the cake.

Section 6

Quick Revision Notes & Common Pitfalls

Exam master tips and fraction calculation shortcuts.

Don't Make This Mistake in Exams:
• Cross-Multiplying vs. Straight Multiplying: When multiplying fractions, multiply straight across: numerator × numerator, and denominator × denominator. Do NOT cross-multiply!
• Flipping the Wrong Fraction in Division: Always flip the SECOND fraction (the divisor), NEVER the first fraction!
• Converting Mixed Numbers First: Always convert mixed numbers like 2 ⅓ into improper fraction 7/3 BEFORE multiplying or dividing!
Teacher's Golden Fraction Rules:
1. Multiplication: (a/b) × (c/d) = (ac)/(bd).
2. Division: Keep first, change to ×, flip second ⇒ (a/b) × (d/c).
3. Reciprocal: Product of a number and its reciprocal is always 1.
4. Cross-cancellation: Cancel common factors before multiplying to keep calculations small and clean!
