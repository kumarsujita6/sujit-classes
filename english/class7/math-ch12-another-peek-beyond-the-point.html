Section 1: Concept Awakening & Visual Intuition

1. Area Model for Decimal Multiplication

How do we visualize 0.3 × 0.4? Take a 1 × 1 unit square. Divide it into 10 equal columns (each 0.1 wide) and 10 equal rows (each 0.1 tall). This creates a 10 × 10 grid of 100 tiny hundredth squares (each 0.01).

2. Shifting the Decimal Point: Powers of 10

Multiplying or dividing by 10, 100, 1000 simply shifts the decimal point by the number of zeros:

• Multiply by 10k: Shift decimal point k places to the RIGHT.
  Example: 24.67 × 10 = 246.7  |  24.67 × 100 = 2467  |  24.67 × 1000 = 24670

• Divide by 10k: Shift decimal point k places to the LEFT.
  Example: 3.9 ÷ 10 = 0.39  |  3.9 ÷ 100 = 0.039  |  3.9 ÷ 1000 = 0.0039

Section 2: Core Theory & Operations

1. The Universal Decimal Multiplication Rule

Step-by-Step Multiplication Algorithm
  1. Step 1: Multiply both numbers ignoring their decimal points (treat them as whole numbers).
  2. Step 2: Count the total number of decimal digits after the decimal point across both factors.
  3. Step 3: In the product, count from the rightmost digit and place the decimal point after that total number of digits. Insert leading zeros if necessary!
Example: 5.96 × 24.8
Whole numbers product: 596 × 248 = 147,808
Decimal places in 5.96 = 2; in 24.8 = 1 ⇒ Total = 2 + 1 = 3 decimal places.
Count 3 digits from right in 147808 ⇒ 147.808

2. Decimal Division: Converting Divisor to Whole Number

Dividing by a decimal divisor can feel tricky. The golden trick is to multiply both dividend and divisor by 10, 100, or 1000 until the divisor becomes a whole number!

Example: 4.68 ÷ 1.3
Multiply both by 10: (4.68 × 10) ÷ (1.3 × 10) = 46.8 ÷ 13
Perform normal long division:
46 ÷ 13 = 3 with remainder 7.
Regroup 7 Ones into 70 Tenths + 8 Tenths = 78 Tenths.
Place decimal point in quotient: 78 Tenths ÷ 13 = 6 Tenths.
Quotient = 3.6

3. Why Does Dividing by a Decimal Produce a Larger Quotient?

Students often ask: "In whole numbers, division makes things smaller (128 ÷ 4 = 32 < 128). Why is 128 ÷ 0.4 = 320 > 128?"

The Fraction Meaning of Division
128 ÷ 0.4 means: "How many 0.4-liter cups can you fill from a 128-liter tank?"
Since each cup holds less than 1 liter (only 4/10 of a liter), you can fill far more cups than the number of liters!
Whenever divisor < 1, Quotient > Dividend!

4. Emil Artin’s Cyclic Numbers: The Magic of 1/7

When you convert 1/7 to a decimal, you get an infinitely repeating block of 6 digits: 1/7 = 0.142857 142857 142857...

Multiply this cyclic block 142857 by 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6:

142857 × 1 = 142857
142857 × 2 = 285714
142857 × 3 = 428571
142857 × 4 = 571428
142857 × 5 = 714285
142857 × 6 = 857142
142857 × 7 = 999999!

Every product up to 6 contains the exact same digits in cyclic order! In 1927, Austrian mathematician Emil Artin conjectured that infinitely many such cyclic primes exist, an unsolved problem to this day.

5. Look Before You Leap! Astronomical Decimals

The Earth does not orbit the Sun in exactly 365 days. It takes approximately 365.2422 solar days!

• Excess time each year = 0.2422 days ≈ 1/4 of a day.
• In 4 years: 4 × 0.2422 = 0.9688 days ≈ 1 extra Leap Day (February 29).
• But adding 1 day every 4 years adds 0.25 days per year, which overcounts by 0.25 − 0.2422 = 0.0078 days per year!
• In 400 years: 400 × 0.0078 = 3.12 days too many!
• That is why century years (like 1700, 1800, 1900) are NOT leap years, unless divisible by 400 (like 1600, 2000, 2400).

Q1: Peanut Chikki vs Potato Chips (Which is Cheaper?)
1. Given:
• Peanut Chikki: 210 g costs ₹70.5
• Potato Chips: 110 g costs ₹33.25
2. Unit Cost Calculation:
Cost of Chikki per gram = 70.5 ÷ 210 ≈ ₹0.3357 / g (or ₹33.57 per 100 g)
Cost of Chips per gram = 33.25 ÷ 110 ≈ ₹0.3023 / g (or ₹30.23 per 100 g)
3. Answer: Since ₹0.3023 < ₹0.3357, the potato chips packet is cheaper per unit weight.

Chemistry Lab Solution Preparation
A laboratory technician needs to divide 1.75 liters of acid into test tubes containing 0.025 liters each. How many test tubes can be completely filled?
View Solution
Number of tubes = 1.75 ÷ 0.025 = (1.75 × 1000) ÷ (0.025 × 1000) = 1750 ÷ 25 = 70 test tubes.
Assertion & Reason (Decimal Magnitude)

Assertion (A): When 48 is divided by 0.6, the quotient is 80, which is greater than 48.

Reason (R): When any positive number is divided by a divisor between 0 and 1, the quotient is always strictly greater than the dividend.

View Solution
Correct Option: Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A. Since 0.6 < 1, dividing 48 by 6/10 is equivalent to multiplying 48 by 10/6 = 80.
