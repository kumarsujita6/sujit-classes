2.1 What is an Arithmetic Expression?

An arithmetic expression is a combination of numbers (operands) connected by operations (+, −, ×, ÷). Every expression has an evaluated value.

  • Expression: 13 + 2 ⇒ Value: 15
  • Expression: 20 − 4 ⇒ Value: 16
  • Expression: 12 × 5 ⇒ Value: 60
  • Expression: 18 ÷ 3 ⇒ Value: 6
Expression vs. Equation:
• 13 + 2 is an expression (a mathematical phrase with no equals sign).
• 13 + 2 = 15 is an equation (a full mathematical sentence asserting equality between two sides).

2.2 Why Do We Need the Order of Operations (BODMAS)?

Consider the expression: 5 + 3 × 2. What is its value?

  • If we add first: (5 + 3) × 2 = 8 × 2 = 16.
  • If we multiply first: 5 + (3 × 2) = 5 + 6 = 11.

2.4 The Distributive Law

Multiplication distributes over addition and subtraction:

• a × (b + c) = a × b + a × c
• a × (b − c) = a × b − a × c
This property is the foundation for fast mental math:
  • 97 × 25: (100 − 3) × 25 = 100 × 25 − 3 × 25 = 2500 − 75 = 2,425.
  • 63 × 18: (53 + 10) × 18 = 53 × 18 + 10 × 18 = 954 + 180 = 1,134.

Activity 1: Fill in the Blanks to Equate Expressions (Page 2)

(a) 13 + 4 = ____ + 6
LHS = 17. RHS = x + 6 ⇒ x = 17 − 6 = 11. ✓ 13 + 4 = 11 + 6.
(b) 22 + ____ = 6 × 5
RHS = 30. LHS = 22 + x ⇒ x = 30 − 22 = 8. ✓ 22 + 8 = 6 × 5.
(c) 8 × ____ = 64 ÷ 2
RHS = 32. LHS = 8 × x ⇒ x = 32 ÷ 8 = 4. ✓ 8 × 4 = 64 ÷ 2.
(d) 34 − ____ = 25
34 − x = 25 ⇒ x = 34 − 25 = 9. ✓ 34 − 9 = 25.

Activity 2: Arrange in Ascending Order (Page 2)

Expressions: (a) 67 − 19, (b) 67 − 20, (c) 35 + 25, (d) 5 × 11, (e) 120 ÷ 3.

Calculations:
• (a) 67 − 19 = 48
• (b) 67 − 20 = 47
• (c) 35 + 25 = 60
• (d) 5 × 11 = 55
• (e) 120 ÷ 3 = 40
✓ Ascending Order: 120 ÷ 3 (40) < 67 − 20 (47) < 67 − 19 (48) < 5 × 11 (55) < 35 + 25 (60).

Activity 3: The Famous Snail Problem (Page 20)

Problem: During daytime, a snail climbs 3 cm up a 10 cm post. At night while asleep, it slips down 2 cm. In how many days will the snail reach the treat at the top?

Teacher Warning: Do not blindly say 10 days! Let us trace day by day:
• Day 1: climbs to 3 cm, slips to 1 cm.
• Day 2: starts at 1 cm, reaches 4 cm, slips to 2 cm.
• Day 3: starts at 2 cm, reaches 5 cm, slips to 3 cm.
• Day 4: starts at 3 cm, reaches 6 cm, slips to 4 cm.
• Day 5: starts at 4 cm, reaches 7 cm, slips to 5 cm.
• Day 6: starts at 5 cm, reaches 8 cm, slips to 6 cm.
• Day 7: starts at 6 cm, reaches 9 cm, slips to 7 cm.
• Day 8 (Daytime): Starts at 7 cm, climbs 3 cm ⇒ 7 + 3 = 10 cm! REACHES THE TOP! It does not slip back because it has already reached the treat!
✓ Answer: The snail reaches the treat on Day 8!

Q1. Comparing Expressions Without Evaluating (Page 19)

Fill in the boxes with ‘<’, ‘>’ or ‘=’ by analysing the structure:

(a) (8 − 3) × 29 _____ (3 − 8) × 29
Reasoning: (8 − 3) = +5 (positive), while (3 − 8) = −5 (negative). A positive number multiplied by 29 is greater than a negative number.
(8 − 3) × 29 > (3 − 8) × 29.
(b) 15 + 9 × 18 _____ (15 + 9) × 18
Reasoning: LHS has only 9 multiplied by 18 plus 15. RHS has both 15 and 9 multiplied by 18 (15 × 18 + 9 × 18). Clearly, 15 + 9 × 18 < 15 × 18 + 9 × 18.
15 + 9 × 18 < (15 + 9) × 18.
(c) 23 × (17 − 9) _____ 23 × 17 + 23 × 9
Reasoning: LHS is 23 × 8. RHS is 23 × 26 (distributive sum).
23 × (17 − 9) < 23 × 17 + 23 × 9.
(d) (34 − 28) × 42 _____ 34 × 42 − 28 × 42
Reasoning: This is the exact distributive property over subtraction. Both sides are identical!
(34 − 28) × 42 = 34 × 42 − 28 × 42.
Q2. Melvin's Story Reading (Page 20)

Problem: Melvin reads a two-page story every day except on Tuesdays and Saturdays. How many stories would he complete reading in 8 weeks?

Given: 7 days in a week. Melvin does NOT read on Tuesdays and Saturdays (2 days excluded).
Reading Days: Days he reads per week = 7 − 2 = 5 days.
Stories per Week: 1 story per reading day ⇒ 5 stories per week.
Total in 8 Weeks: 8 × (7 − 2) × 1 = 8 × 5 = 40 stories.
✓ Answer: Melvin completes 40 stories in 8 weeks. (Expression: 8 × (7 − 2)).
Q3. Binu's Annual Savings (Page 20)

Problem: Binu earns ₹20,000 per month. She spends ₹5,000 on rent, ₹5,000 on food, and ₹2,000 on other expenses every month. What is the amount Binu will save by the end of a year?

Monthly Income: ₹20,000
Monthly Expenses: ₹5,000 + ₹5,000 + ₹2,000 = ₹12,000
Monthly Savings: ₹20,000 − ₹12,000 = ₹8,000
Annual Savings: 12 × [20,000 − (5,000 + 5,000 + 2,000)] = 12 × 8,000 = ₹96,000.
✓ Answer: Binu saves ₹96,000 by the end of the year.

Correct Answer: (b) 8. Multiply first: 3 × 4 = 12. Then left to right: 18 − 12 = 6, and 6 + 2 = 8!
Q2: Which property of operations allows us to write 25 × 104 as (25 × 100) + (25 × 4)?
Teacher Explanation
Correct Answer: (c). 25 × (100 + 4) = 25 × 100 + 25 × 4 is the Distributive Property.
Q3: Evaluate: [30 − {12 − (8 − 3)}]
Teacher Explanation
Correct Answer: (a) 23.
1. Innermost round brackets: (8 − 3) = 5.
2. Curly brackets: {12 − 5} = 7.
3. Square brackets: 30 − 7 = 23!
Q4: Which expression correctly represents: "5 subtracted from the product of 8 and 3"?
Teacher Explanation
Correct Answer: (d) 8 × 3 − 5. The product of 8 and 3 is 8 × 3. Subtracting 5 from it gives (8 × 3) − 5.
Q5: What is 98 × 45 calculated using the distributive law?
Teacher Explanation
Correct Answer: (b) 4,410. 98 × 45 = (100 − 2) × 45 = 100 × 45 − 2 × 45 = 4500 − 90 = 4,410!

Quick Revision Notes & Common Pitfalls

Exam master tips and golden rules.

Common Exam Mistakes to Avoid:
• Left-to-Right Blind Evaluation: Doing 10 − 3 × 2 as 7 × 2 = 14 is completely wrong! Always multiply first: 10 − 6 = 4.
• Bracket Subtraction Sign Distribution: When opening a minus bracket, signs change: a − (b + c) = a − b − c (NOT a − b + c!).
• Confusing "Subtracted from": "x subtracted from y" is y − x, NOT x − y.
Teacher's Golden Summary:
1. BODMAS Order: Brackets → Orders → Division/Multiplication → Addition/Subtraction.
2. Brackets grouping order: ( ) → { } → [ ].
3. Distributive property speeds up mental math: a(b ± c) = ab ± ac.
4. Expressions describe relationships and real-world situations concisely!
